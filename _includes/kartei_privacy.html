Die Kartei — Privacy Policy

May 18, 2026 · Version 1.0

Die Kartei is designed with privacy as a core principle. All AI processing happens entirely on your device — no data is sent to external servers, no cloud processing occurs, and no account is required.

Data Collection

Die Kartei does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. This includes:

  • Flashcard decks and study content you create
  • German vocabulary, phrases, and learning progress
  • AI-generated flashcard content and translations
  • Model selections and app preferences
  • Device usage and performance metrics

On-Device Processing

All AI inference, flashcard generation, and German language processing runs locally on your iPhone using on-device models. No internet connection is required after initial model downloads.

Network Usage

The only network activity Die Kartei performs is downloading AI models from their respective providers when you explicitly choose to download a model. No user data is transmitted during this process.

Third-Party Services

Die Kartei does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

All app data — including flashcard decks, study history, and downloaded models — is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Die Kartei does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL. Since Die Kartei collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
