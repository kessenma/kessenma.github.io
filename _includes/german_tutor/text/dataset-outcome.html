Final dataset: 960 targeted examples (816 corrections, 94 tutor dialogues across freestyle, formal-Sie, role-play and vocabulary-weaving modes, and 50 flashcard-JSON examples) blended with ~35% general German instruction data to prevent catastrophic forgetting. About 1,450 training examples total. Small, but every one of them aimed at a measured failure.

Total cash spent on data: roughly $0 — generation ran through my coding-assistant subscription's agents, grammar validation was self-hosted, and the only API involved (DeepL) used about 0.5% of its free tier.
