The through-line that ties all the tools together into one coach. It's not a growing chat log — it's a small, bounded profile stored on-device: a struggle rating for each of ~10 grammar structures, the last 60 words you're building, and the last 20 slip-ups it's watching (which self-heal once you use the word correctly). Old items get archived, never deleted, and the archive never touches a prompt. The model itself only ever sees a ~60-token briefing — the top slice by struggle and recency — injected into the conversation and correction prompts. You can see everything it knows in Coach's Notes, pin or remove items, or reset it. The Today screen turns the same profile into a daily plan ("Review 5 due · 3-min drill on Dativ · 5-min chat"), and due vocabulary gets quietly woven into conversations — the prompt says to create openings for those words without announcing it.
