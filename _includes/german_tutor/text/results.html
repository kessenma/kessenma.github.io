Results

Same eval, same automated scoring, both models as the 4-bit MLX quantizations that actually ship on a phone. The table below is really three experiments stacked on one bench, and it pays to read it as three arms of a controlled trial:

  • The core-four suite is the treatment arm. The four areas the dataset actually targets — where a gain is expected, and therefore proves the least.
  • The ten-area extension suite is the side-effect arm. Grammar the dataset never touches, so the only interesting outcome is a drop — that would be catastrophic forgetting showing up.
  • The 32 already-correct sentences are the placebo arm. The right answer is to do nothing. A tutor that "fixes" a sentence you got right is worse than one that stays quiet, and this is the only slice that catches it — an accuracy number alone would have called the stock model a 72% success while it was quietly rewriting a third of the things its student got right.

Both columns score the raw checkpoint — the strictest possible view. A later section ("The eval was lying") re-scores everything the way the app actually renders corrections; the shape of this comparison survives, and the shipped numbers improve.

Four things I read off this table:

  1. The behavioral metrics moved most, and they're the ones that matter. Missed errors halved (17% → 9%), false corrections dropped by a third (34% → 22%). That was the thesis of the whole dataset design — verdict discipline over grammar trivia — and it paid off.
  2. The knowledge areas the model half-knew went to (near-)perfect. Verbs with prepositions 15/15, separable verbs 14/15. Where the base model already had the substrate, 19 minutes of QLoRA finished the job.
  3. Reflexives barely moved (60% → 67%). The accusative/dative reflexive distinction (ich wasche mich vs. ich wasche mir die Hände) appears to be genuinely hard, not just under-trained. That slice gets a dedicated second data wave.
  4. The untargeted extension suite went up, not down (87% → 90%). The ~35% general instruction mix-in did its anti-forgetting job; nothing was traded away.
