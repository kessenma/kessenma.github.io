Where it stands

Two fine-tuned models are live in the app — "Gemma 4 E4B German Tutor" on recent phones and "Gemma 4 E2B German Tutor" on the 6 GB tier — with both sets of 4-bit weights public on Hugging Face (kessenma/gemma4-e4b-german-tutor-4bit, kessenma/gemma4-e2b-german-tutor-4bit) along with the training data, both eval suites, the source lexicons and every eval result. Total cost of the project: roughly $30 of GPU time end-to-end — about $6 of it generating the v2 corpus, a dollar for the original fine-tunes, ~$3 regenerating that corpus behind a dense teacher after the first one turned out to share the students' capacity problem, ~$10 for the four-base sweep that finally cracked the 4 GB tier, and an embarrassing ~$3 of a pod idling overnight because my shutdown failsafe wedged on a hung SSH poll. Zero API spend, and a lot of reading synthetic German.

Next up:

  • Decide E4B v4 vs v1 — the corpus rebuild bought false corrections and a far more natural voice at the cost of a higher miss rate, and the grammar difference is a statistical tie (p = 1.00). That's a temperament call, not a scoreboard call.
